## 2. What this tells us

1. **Everyone sells a commodity.** The ads are about price ($199, $299, $66/mo, free), speed (30 seconds, 7 days), or "you need a website." Almost none show the work.
2. **Price is a race you can't win.** $199 and free are the floor. At $399 we lose any ad that leads with price.
3. **"Free" works when it has proof.** The Free Website Guys have run the same angle for over a year. Their strength is volume proof, which we don't have yet.
4. **Only one competitor offers a free mockup** (Hello Day One), and it shows nothing. **Showing the transformation on screen is the open lane.**
5. **Monthly plans are expensive.** $99/mo is $1,188 a year, forever. We're $399 once. That's a strong comparison line.
6. **What works for local owners:** a real face, a specific trade, a specific number, and an "is this you?" diagnostic.

**Our position:** *The only ad in the feed that shows a boring local site turning into one people remember, then offers to do yours free before you pay anything.*

Honesty rule: we have no clients yet. Call the 7 sites **concept sites / samples**, never fake testimonials or lead numbers. Proof comes from showing the work.

### Hook variations (film all, test 3–5 at once, same body)

1. **Before/after (default):** "This is a glass shop's website. This is the one I built them."
2. **Call-out:** "If you own a roofing company in Vegas, your website looks like this." (show a generic before). Make a version for each trade: roofers, groomers, landscapers, restaurants, cleaners.
3. **Price contrast:** "Stop paying $99 a month for a website you don't even like."
4. **Diagnostic (Owner.com style):** "Google your business right now. If your site looks like this, you're losing the call to the guy next door."
5. **Challenge:** "Give me your business name and 48 hours. I'll redesign your homepage for free."

### Cutdowns

- **15s (Reels/Stories):** hook (3s) → 3 fast after-sites (6s) → "Free homepage mockup in 48 hours. Tap to send your business name." (6s) → koi card.
- **30s:** hook → problem line → 2 after-sites → offer → CTA.
- **Static/carousel:** before/after pairs, one trade per card, final card "Yours next? Free mockup."

## 5. Must-fix before spending

- **Landing page must match the ad.** The ad promises a *free* mockup; the site currently leads with the $100 deposit. Change the hero and button to "Get my free mockup" (copy in `FACEBOOK_AND_SITE.md`, section 3). Move the $100 deposit to after they've seen the mockup.
- **Form = business name + email + trade/city.** Nothing else. Every extra field costs leads.
- **Speed is the product.** Reply within an hour and deliver the mockup in 48 hours. Paste names to me and I'll build them the same day.

## 6. Campaign setup

- Objective: **Leads** (instant form) or **Sales/Leads** to the site if the pixel is installed. Start with an instant form because it's cheaper per lead for a free offer.
- Audiences: Nationwide US, broad (Advantage+), ages 28–65, plus a Las Vegas/Henderson ad set with a local hook ("Henderson business owners…").
- Budget: $20–30/day per ad set, 3–5 creatives (hook variations), 5–7 days before judging. Kill anything over ~$25 per lead after $75 spent; scale winners by 20% every 2 days.
- Metrics: hook rate (3s views ÷ impressions) above 30%, CTR above 1%, cost per mockup request, and mockup-to-deposit rate.
