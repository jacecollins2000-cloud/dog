# References — GC homepage v3 (2026-09-29)

Discovery: Firecrawl search/scrape of Awwwards (Fashion, Sports), Siteinspire (Fashion), Refero Styles, plus direct brand sites.
Inspection: each candidate rendered in Chromium at 1440×900 and 390×844 (iPhone UA), 5–16 scroll frames per viewport.
Frames live in the session scratchpad (`refs/`, `shots/ref-*.png`); they are third-party screenshots and are not committed.

## Chosen

### Primary — day one® Run (https://day1-run.webflow.io/)
Awwwards Honorable Mention, 29 Jun 2026 (Sports / E-commerce). A sports brand's community hub: mission, community runs, product.
Why primary: same problem as GC — an athletic brand whose product sits inside a mission and a community. Demanding on type scale, pacing and composition.
Mechanisms observed in the rendered site:
- **Echo headline.** "Uniting Runners." bright white, "City by City." directly under it in dim grey. One family, hierarchy by tone.
- **Rail label + large statement.** "Our Mission" small and grey on the left rail; the mission itself set ~30px across the right two-thirds. No image in that beat.
- **Giant word over a cropped photograph.** "Calendar" and "Pants" set at ~9vw, anchored left, crossing into a photo that bleeds right.
- **One hand-drawn red accent** ("You vs. You", "Gear Up") stamped over type — a single human gesture, used sparingly.
- **Quiet centred statement on black** between image blocks ("May our performance attire…").
- **Product row:** four garments on black, name + price in small type, one featured product beside a category tile.
- **Sign-off:** dim "Ready?" over a giant white wordmark and one action.
- Mobile re-stacks rather than shrinks: headlines stay ~48px, the giant category word stays over its image, statement blocks keep their scale.

### Supporting A — Represent (https://representclo.com/) — role: premium restraint + two-worlds split
The brand brief names Represent as half of GC's territory ("Represent meets Nike with a mission").
- Desktop hero is a split: "British Luxury Menswear / Represent" beside "On A Mission / 247 Activewear", each half a full-height monochrome studio photo with a small label, a title and two small buttons.
- Mobile stacks the halves, each about half the screen, same label/title/buttons.
- UI chrome is tiny and quiet; the photography carries the premium.
Borrowed: the split as a way to put two ideas in one screen (GC's individual / troop), small restrained UI over big monochrome photography.

### Supporting B — Nike (https://www.nike.com/) — role: path to product
- "EVERYTHING LED HERE" / "EVERY. DAY. SPEED.": stacked condensed uppercase at the lower left of a full-bleed photo, one line of support, one pill action.
- "NOTHING SPECIAL TO SEE HERE*": a centred headline on white with no image — the quiet beat between loud image blocks.
- Product shown with name and price immediately; on mobile the same headline+action pattern at full width.
Borrowed: one action per image block; product name/price/action legible without hunting.

## Inspected, not chosen
- **Serotoninn** (Awwwards; previous run's primary). Grunge-glam editorial: torn paper, brush buttons, red accents. The current GC build inherited many of these conventions; this run deliberately rebalances toward the brief's "Grit × Premium".
- **Satisfy Running** — strong campaign imagery and product grids; commerce-first, less narrative.
- **Pas Normal Studios** — full-bleed ride video, clean statements; cycling-club register, strong but generic homepage pattern.
- **Fear of God** — full-bleed campaigns, restrained serif; premium but too quiet for GC's athletic edge.
- **Cole Buxton** — campaign + product rails; commerce pattern.
- **Aimé Leon Dore** — single still-life hero at capture time; little to learn.
- **On**, **Achilles Heel** — athletic retail patterns; product-grid heavy.
- **The Last Dance for Glory**, **The Immortals** (Awwwards Sports) — cinematic storytelling microsites; spectacle over product.
- **Offform** (bot wall) and **Brandblack** (store offline) could not be inspected.
