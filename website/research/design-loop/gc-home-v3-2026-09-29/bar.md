# Bar — GC homepage v3

Primary: day one® Run. Supporting: Represent (restraint, split), Nike (path to product). Evidence in `references.md`.
Each criterion must be observable in rendered screenshots at 1440×900 and 390×844 (and not broken at 320×640).

1. **One beat, then the product.** *(Nike: stacked headline + one action over the photo.)*
   After the lights-on opening, the first viewport holds the slogan as the dominant type, one action to the hoodie, and nothing else competing.
   The next scroll step brings the hoodie in with its image, name, example price, sizes and add-to-bag all visible in one desktop viewport.

2. **Echo headlines, one voice.** *(day one: "Uniting Runners." bright / "City by City." dim; small grey rail label.)*
   Every chapter opens with at most one small mono label and one statement, and some statements carry a dimmed echo line.
   No chapter has more than one primary action. No two chapters share the same heading composition.

3. **Giant word meets cropped image.** *(day one: "Calendar", "Pants" over photographs.)*
   At least two chapters set a word or short phrase at ≥ 11vw on desktop, touching or crossing a deliberately cropped photograph.
   On a 390px phone the word stays ≥ 17vw and the crop is recomposed for portrait, not letterboxed.

4. **Quiet before loud; texture as punctuation.** *(day one's centred statement on black; Nike's "Nothing special to see here*".)*
   At least one full-viewport beat has ≤ 30 words, no photograph and no texture, and sits directly before a full-bleed image chapter.
   Torn paper, tape, grain and concrete appear only at a named few moments (≤ 3 on the homepage), never as a repeated section divider.

5. **The individual and the troop in one screen.** *(Represent: two halves side by side, stacked halves on mobile.)*
   One chapter shows "you" and "your people" as two equal halves on desktop and two stacked halves on mobile.
   Each half carries a label and a statement, so GC's two ideas — individuality and belonging — read in a single viewport.

6. **Commerce you can trust.** *(Nike / day one product rows: name, price, action, no hunting.)*
   Concept and demo disclosures sit beside the price and action, and product actions are plain, high-contrast controls with ≥ 44px targets.
   The mobile purchase dock appears only over the product.
   Size → bag → demo checkout completes in ≤ 4 taps on phone and desktop.

7. **A sign-off that lands.** *(day one: dim "Ready?" + giant wordmark + one action.)*
   The page ends on the supplied hand-painted "Live Different!!" artwork as the largest brand moment, uncropped at 320px, followed by one path back to the hoodie.
   The approved gorilla mark appears at least once on a surface where it is fully legible.
